Showcase Privacy Policy

Applies to the Showcase app on Android and iOS. Last updated: 12 November 2026.

Showcase is a demonstration app built on an open-source app foundation. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question or a request, write to mblminds@gmail.com.

Data we collect

WhatWhyWhere it goes
Your account: email address, first and last name, and your password (stored only as a secure hash) To let you sign in and use your profile Our server
Usage events: screens viewed, sign-in, sign-up, sign-out, searches and items viewed, together with device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language, approximate region) and an app-instance identifier To understand how the app is used and improve it Google Firebase Analytics
Crash reports: the error and its stack trace, device model, operating system and app version To find and fix crashes Google Firebase Crashlytics
A Firebase installation identifier To download the app's remote settings (for example, wording or feature limits) Google Firebase Remote Config
Your privacy choices To remember what you agreed to Stored on your device by Google's consent tool

Usage events never include your email, your name, a password or anything you type into the app.

Your consent

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent on first launch, using Google's consent tool.
  • No usage events or crash reports are sent before that question is answered.
  • If you decline, the app still counts basic usage without identifiers, and crash reports stay on so we can keep the app working. Nothing is used for advertising.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings › Privacy choices. A change applies immediately.

Advertising

Showcase shows no ads and does not sell your data. The consent tool is provided by Google AdMob, which is why AdMob appears in its messages.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers listed above (Google Firebase and Google's consent tool), who process it on our behalf. Their handling is described in Firebase's privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy. We do not sell or rent your data.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account exists.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months, Firebase's default retention.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days, Crashlytics' default retention.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to mblminds@gmail.com. Deleting your account deletes the account data on our server. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

Showcase is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date above.
